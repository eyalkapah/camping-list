# The app organises by category; the export stays organised by person

The group has read the same per-person numbered WhatsApp message for years, and that format is
how they recognise "the list". But shopping and gap analysis are category questions that a
per-person list cannot answer. We made category the primary structure in the app, with a
per-person view as a filter over the same Items, and made the WhatsApp export render in the
familiar per-person numbered shape with the gaps appended.

## Consequences

The export deliberately does not resemble the app's primary view. This is not an oversight and
should not be "fixed" — the export's job is to be unremarkable to people who never open the app.
